How It Works & IRCTC Rules

❓

Why does an earlier station have confirmed seats?

Indian Railways uses pooled quotas and station-wise quotas. Major origin hubs are allocated a large bulk of berths (General Quota), whereas intermediate remote stops often share a much smaller pooled quota (PQWL) or remote quota (RLWL) that fills up quickly.

📋

Will the Ticket Examiner (TTE) give away my seat?

Not if you set your Boarding Point! IRCTC officially allows travelers to book from station A while setting their boarding point at station B. The train chart will officially list you as boarding at B, so your berth is preserved and protected until your actual boarding station.

💳

How do I book this on IRCTC?

Search the journey on IRCTC using the earlier confirmed station as the source station. On the passenger details page, click "Change Boarding Station" and choose your original station. Pay for the ticket—done!
